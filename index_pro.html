1933

The shop opens at Tenkasi

P. S. Pichandi Chettiar, then twenty-three, starts a cycle shop on Swami Sannathi. Within ten years the firm is well known across the Indian cycle industry.

1950s

The branch years

The business extends to Tirunelveli, Tuticorin, Rajapalayam and Palghat. Invoices from Ambadi Enterprises, Atlas, Hero, Avon and Dunlop fill the ledgers.

16 December 1960

T. I. Cycle Dealers of Tamilnad, Coimbatore

The firm takes its place among the dealers received by Sri A. M. M. Murugappa Chettiar — the beginning of a long association with Tube Investments of India.

1970

A dealer abroad

The founder travels widely and visits the U.S.A. Five years later he is at the Hero Cycles works in Ludhiana with the dealers' delegation.

1983–84

Golden Jubilee

Fifty years of trading. The second generation continues at Tenkasi while the founder's brothers-in-trade run the other towns. P. S. Pichandi Chettiar dies the same year.

1990

The Shiromani Award

The Shiromani Awards Institute, New Delhi, honours the founder for his contribution to the cycle trade and to national economic development.

1996 — today

The third generation

Mr. P. Sathish B.E. runs the shop at Swami Sannathi. Geared, disc-brake and electric cycles now stand where the roadsters did — sold and serviced over the same counter.

“We are in cycle trade for the past 59 years. Our firm was founded in the year 1933… I have ambition to run the trade for more than 100 years.”
P. Sathish — letter to TI Chakra, 25 June 1992
